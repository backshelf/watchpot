# Orbital Orchard

The game's concept as `/watchpot` took it down. The answers themselves are in `answers.json` beside this file.

## Fiction

A lone caretaker on a derelict space station grows fruit trees in orbit for a planet that stopped answering. The fruit keeps coming. Somebody should be there to pick it.

**Voice.** Quiet, dry, a little hopeful. Short lines, said to nobody in particular. No exclamation marks; the jokes are deadpan and the hope is understated.

**Who plays.** Someone with a desk and a spare ten minutes, a handful of times a day: checks the orchard, plants, grafts, and leaves it to grow.

## Loop

- **Orchard.** 5 generators make sunlight: Dwarf apple, Pressure plum, Hull fig, Vacuum cherry, Lagrange pear.
- **Modules.** An upgrade tree (Station modules) of 6 nodes, bought with sunlight.
- **Grafts.** Graft kit pulls for seeds, a ladder of Common < Rare < Exotic < Heirloom, 8 things to find; every copy owned adds its bonus.
- **Shower.** A meteor shower two days in every three, outside the run: nets (back on their own, and from planting)
  cast at a sky of twelve patches catch shards, a seed pod or nothing; shards mend the nets; the catch pays seeds,
  sunlight and a free graft kit at its milestones.
- **Orbit.** Change orbit resets the run for Orbits, each one raising yield for good.

Stats: Yield (output), Night shift (offline share), Delta-v (prestige gain).

## Session

Built for short sessions: a handful a day, about ten minutes each. The pacing contract is `src/content/pacing.ts`.

- Type: Fraunces (display), Work Sans (interface), JetBrains Mono (numbers), from Google Fonts.
- **The moment.** The first heirloom graft blooms: the bench goes dark, one bud swells in rose light on the stage, and every pane of the station's glass catches it.
