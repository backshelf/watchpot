# Shaders: the outline

The shaders section: interactive pages on `watchpot/shader` and `watchpot/three` for a reader who wants a look of their
own. One idea a page, on the same terms as getting started: a fragment shader behind a screen, uniforms the game sets,
the pool of canvases, the GLSL chunks, and the 3D stage. The examples are Simmer's own shaders and a small 3D scene.
Nothing is written yet: this paragraph and the headings below are the plan.

**This file is read by the build**: each `## N. Title` heading is a page, in order. A page that exists is
`pages/NN-slug.tsx` and its `title` must match its heading here (a test checks it); a heading with no page yet is
drawn in the progress strip as a step still to come. Keep the headings' form.
